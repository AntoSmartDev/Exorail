# Roadmap

The authoritative work tree is Epic → Feature → User Story → Task. Milestones
collect Stories and Contexts tag work. Use projections for cross-tree planning.

| Priority | Epic | Feature | User Story | Status | Dependencies | Milestone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | `EP-...` | `FEAT-...` | `US-...` | planned | | `MS-...` |

Do not reserve legacy identifiers or use roadmap entries as implementation
authority. A specific Story owns its Task backlog.
